FIN-002 — Bond Outflow Stress

Rule Information

Rule ID: FIN-002
Domain: Financial Resilience
Category: Sovereign Bond Market
Status: Active
Version: 1.0
Repository: Sextant Rule Library
Purpose

Evaluate the resilience of the financial system during periods of significant foreign investor withdrawals from sovereign bond markets and assess potential cascading effects across connected domains.

Scenario

A sustained sell-off of government bonds by domestic or foreign investors reduces market liquidity and increases borrowing costs.

The scenario evaluates:

Bond market liquidity
Government bond yields
Foreign investor participation
Capital outflows
Financial market confidence
Primary Indicators

Government bond yields
Foreign investor bond holdings
Daily bond trading volume
Bond market volatility
Capital outflow trends
Credit spread movements
Risk Levels

GREEN

Normal bond market conditions.

Liquidity is stable and market confidence remains strong.

Action: No contingency actions required.

YELLOW

Early signs of reduced bond market liquidity.

Monitor:

Bond yields
Foreign investor activity
Trading volumes
Action: Increase monitoring frequency and review liquidity conditions.

ORANGE

Significant bond outflows causing market stress.

Indicators:

Rapid increase in bond yields
Accelerating foreign investor withdrawals
Reduced market liquidity
Increased market volatility
Potential Cascade:

«Bond Outflow → Higher Borrowing Costs → Liquidity Tightening → Financial Market Stress»

Action: Prepare contingency measures and enhance market surveillance.

RED

Severe bond market disruption.

Indicators:

Extreme bond yield movements
Major investor withdrawals
Liquidity breakdown
Broad financial instability
Potential Cascade:

«Bond Market Crisis → Funding Stress → Banking Sector Pressure → Systemic Financial Risk»

Action: Activate emergency response procedures and systemic financial stability measures, subject to human authorization.

Cross-Domain Dependencies

Financial (FIN) ↓ Infrastructure (INF) ↓ Cyber (CYB) ↓ Data Centre Operations (DC)

Financial market instability may increase operational stress across interconnected domains.

The FIN Rule Engine identifies affected domains but does not independently execute actions in another domain.

Cross-domain execution remains subject to that domain's rule engine and the SPD human authorization framework.

Contingency Actions

Monitor government bond markets continuously
Assess liquidity conditions
Review funding requirements
Evaluate systemic contagion risks
Increase reporting frequency
Escalate to institutional risk management when thresholds are exceeded
