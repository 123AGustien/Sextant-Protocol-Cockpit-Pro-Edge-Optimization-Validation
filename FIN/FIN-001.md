FIN-001 — FX Stress

Rule Information

Rule ID: FIN-001
Domain: Financial Resilience
Category: Foreign Exchange
Status: Active
Version: 1.0
Repository: Sextant Rule Library
Purpose

Evaluate the resilience of the financial system during periods of significant foreign exchange stress and assess potential cascading effects across connected financial and operational domains.

Scenario

A rapid or sustained movement in foreign exchange markets creates financial stress through currency volatility, capital-flow changes, liquidity pressure, increased funding costs, or declining market confidence.

The scenario evaluates:

Exchange-rate volatility
Capital flows
Foreign reserve pressure
Sovereign and bond-market conditions
Liquidity conditions
Financial market confidence
Primary Indicators

Exchange-rate volatility
Currency depreciation or appreciation
Foreign capital flows
Foreign reserve levels
Sovereign bond-market activity
Market liquidity
Funding spreads
Financial market confidence
Risk Levels

GREEN

Normal foreign exchange market conditions.

Currency markets remain orderly, liquidity is stable, and financial market confidence remains strong.

Action: No contingency actions required.

YELLOW

Early signs of foreign exchange pressure.

Monitor:

Exchange-rate movements
Capital flows
Foreign reserve conditions
Market liquidity
Funding spreads
Action: Increase monitoring frequency and review foreign exchange and liquidity conditions.

ORANGE

Significant foreign exchange stress.

Indicators:

Rapid currency movements
Accelerating capital outflows
Increasing funding costs
Reduced market liquidity
Rising financial market volatility
Potential Cascade:

«FX Stress → Inflation Pressure → Interest Rate Pressure → Liquidity Tightening → Financial Market Stress»

Action: Prepare contingency measures, strengthen financial surveillance, and assess potential cross-domain effects.

RED

Severe foreign exchange disruption.

Indicators:

Extreme currency movements
Major capital outflows
Significant reserve pressure
Liquidity deterioration
Broad financial market instability
Potential Cascade:

«FX Crisis → Funding Stress → Liquidity Disruption → Financial Market Instability → Systemic Financial Risk»

Action: Escalate to institutional risk management and activate appropriate emergency financial resilience procedures subject to human authorization.

Cross-Domain Dependencies

Financial (FIN) ↓ Infrastructure (INF) ↓ Cyber (CYB) ↓ Data Centre Operations (DC)

Foreign exchange stress may increase financial, operational, and systemic pressure across interconnected domains.

The FIN rule engine identifies affected domains but does not independently execute actions within another domain.

Cross-domain execution remains subject to the relevant domain rule engine and the SPD human authorization framework.

Contingency Actions

Monitor foreign exchange markets continuously
Assess currency volatility
Review capital-flow conditions
Assess foreign reserve pressure
Review liquidity and funding conditions
Evaluate systemic contagion risks
Increase reporting frequency
Escalate to institutional risk management when thresholds are exceeded
